Wergonic · Admin dashboard · Design changes

Changes to the
admin prototype

The prototype covers the product definition well and most of it needs nothing. This is the list of what to change, after clicking through every screen and checking it against what the system actually does. Several items are here because the design shows something that does not exist — languages, industries, a quota — and those matter most, because a developer would build them.

7 September 2026 · reviewed wergonic-admin-dashboard.vercel.app
Against Wergonic Admin — Product Definition, version 2, which has been updated with all of this.
First, what is right

Most of the tool does not need touching.

Home reads exactly as intended. The confirmation dialogs are genuinely good — "a real email goes to a real customer" is the right instinct. Suspend, archive, delete, approve and reject all confirm properly and say what will happen. The over-limit and expiring-soon colouring on the organizations list works. Releases is well judged, including leaving Save disabled until something changes. The company page shape is right. None of that is in this list.

A · Things shown that do not exist4 changes

A1The translation languages are wrong

Translations

The page shows English, Swedish, German, Danish, Norwegian and Polish. The product has English, Swedish, German, Spanish and Dutch. Danish, Norwegian and Polish do not exist anywhere in it, and two real languages are missing.

Replace the six with those five, in the completeness bars, the export picker and the editor table.

A2The industries are invented

Organizations list, filters, company overview, approval cards

Automotive, Food processing, Consulting, Warehousing, Electronics, Pharmaceuticals and Software are not options in the system. Industry is a fixed official classification of 21 categories — Manufacturing, Transportation and Storage, Human Health and Social Work Activities, Construction, and so on.

Those names are much longer, so the column and the filter need to hold longer text than the mock data does. Worth checking the column does not break with "Wholesale and Retail Trade; Repair of Motor Vehicles and Motorcycles" in it.

A3AI usage has no limit

Company page → Plan & usage

It is shown as "61 / 250" and "210 / 500". There is no AI quota — nothing caps it and nothing enforces it. The four real limits are people, admins, recordings per month and assessments per month, and those are all correct as designed.

Show AI usage as a plain count with the change against last month, like the platform numbers on Home. No bar, no ceiling.

A4Archive cannot be reached from Active

Company page → header and Danger zone

Both places offer Archive on an active company. The system only allows Active → Suspended, then Suspended → Archived. You have to suspend first.

Show only the moves that are possible from the state the company is in. On an active company that is Suspend; Archive appears once it is suspended.

B · Things that need a control4 changes

B1Changing a role happens with one click and no confirmation

Person page → Memberships, and company page → People

Picking a different role in the dropdown applies it immediately. Every other consequential action in the tool confirms properly — this is the only one that does not, and it decides what someone can do inside a customer's account.

Confirm it the way the others do: name the person, the company, the role they hold now and the role they will hold.

B2Support has an Owner column that nothing can fill

Support list and message page

The column exists and the mock data has names in it, but there is no way to set one. The message page says "owned by nobody yet" and offers only Close and Send reply.

One button on the message: Take it, which puts your name in the column. Everyone on the team has the same access, so there is nobody else to assign to. Add "owned by me" to the list filters.

B3Invitations can be seen but not acted on

Person page and company page → People

A pending invitation shows with a badge, and the Expired state is designed properly. But there is no way to resend it, cancel it, or copy the link — and those are exactly what support is asked for when someone says they never got the email. The customer product already has all three.

Add Resend, Copy link and Cancel on each pending invitation, in both places.

B4There is no sign out

Profile menu

The menu shows who you are and the light/dark toggle. It needs a sign out.

While that menu is open: the "Preview empty states" toggle must come out. It was a useful way to show the empty screens during the review, but it cannot be in the real tool.

C · Things that say nothing3 changes

C1An empty invitations block still gets a heading and a sentence

Person page and company page → People

On a person who accepted their invitation long ago, the page still prints "Invitations sent to j.lindqvist@volvo.com" and underneath it "No invitations on record for this address." The company People tab does the same with "No open invitations — everyone who was invited has accepted". That is a heading and a sentence spent telling someone that nothing happened.

Do not draw the block at all when there is nothing in it. The rule for the whole tool: an empty state is only worth designing when the emptiness is the news — an empty repair queue is good news and deserves its screen; "no invitations" is not news.

C2The assessments tab has no filters, the recordings tab has three

Health

Recordings can be narrowed by problem, company and date range. Assessments cannot be narrowed at all, so on a bad day it is just a list.

Give it company, date range, assessment type (RAMP, MEC, RULA, REBA) and state. An assessment has only two failure states — failed, or stuck part-finished — so the state filter is shorter than the recordings one, not missing. The assessments rows should also get the tick boxes for bulk actions that the recordings rows have.

C3Reject is an unlabelled cross

Organizations → waiting for approval

The confirmation behind it is well written. But rejecting an application permanently deletes it, and the only way in is a small X in the corner of the card, with no label, next to a large black Approve button. The two decisions carry similar weight and look nothing alike.

Make Reject a plain labelled button beside Approve, quieter in style but readable as a choice.

D · Wording and detail5 changes

D1The Approve dialog describes a flow we no longer have

Organizations → waiting for approval

It says approving "creates the company's admin account, emails them their login". That changed: the system now sends an invitation, and the person sets their own name and password on the accept page. No password is generated and none is emailed.

Reword to match. Everything else about the dialog is right, including saying out loud that a real email goes to a real customer.

D2The app version screen should say where those numbers live

Releases → mobile app version control

The minimum and latest version are not stored with everything else — the mobile app reads them from a separate configuration document and checks them when it launches. That is invisible on the screen, and a developer building this would reasonably wire it to the wrong place.

A line on the screen saying these are the values the app reads on launch. The warning treatment around it is already right.

D3The reply box should say where the reply goes

Support → message page

There is no support inbox in the customer product, so a reply can only be an email to the person who wrote in. That changes how someone writes it, and right now nothing on the page says so.

A line above the box: this is sent as an email to their address. And keep it as one message and one reply — not a thread. Their answer comes back to the mailbox, not into this tool.

D4Sensor ids run together

Devices & sensors, and company page → Devices

The sensors column reads S-7F21S-7F22S-7F23S-7F24. They need to be separate items — chips, or spaced — in both places.

D5Small ones

Various

  • Users list: the column header says EMAIL but the cell says "Verified". Either rename the column or show the address in it.
  • Users list: make sure "Wergonic admin" is one of the values in the role filter. That filter is how we answer who at Wergonic can open the tool.
  • Devices register: no search and no filters at all. The register should be searchable by serial and sensor id, and filterable by company.
  • Support: only Close exists. Nothing moves a message from New to Open, and a closed one cannot be reopened.
  • Company page: Suspend and Archive are in the header and again in the Danger zone. Pick one place.
  • Recording detail: it should open for any recording, not only a broken one — global search offers recording numbers and most questions are about one that is fine.

E · The hardware register — simplified1 change

E1Make it read-only

Devices & sensors, and the device page

"Attach sensor" is greyed out on every device, including one with no sensors, with no reason given. Detach is designed. There is also no way to add a device.

The decision is to drop all of it. The register lists what we have and who has it — serial, model, company, person, the sensors on it, when it was added. Nothing on this screen changes anything. A serial appears on its own the first time a recording is made with it, so nothing needs adding by hand.

"In stock at Wergonic" stays as it is. It reads as a status but it is really just the company column when no company is assigned, which is the right way to show it.

One thing to know

What a "device" is has not been settled yet.

The design shows a hub with sensors attached to it, which is how the kit physically works. In the system today, a "device" record is created automatically from the serial the phone reports when a recording starts — and that is a sensor serial, not a hub. There is a second, separate list of sensors that is filled by hand and is effectively empty.

This does not block anything in the design; the register looks the same either way. It is flagged so nobody is surprised when the two do not line up during the build.
